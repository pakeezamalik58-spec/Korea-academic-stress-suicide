# Academic Stress and Suicide Risk Among South Korean Adolescents

Stata code for my Master of Public Policy thesis at the KDI School of Public Policy and Management (2026).

**Research question:** Does the start of the Korean academic year on March 2 increase suicide risk among students?

**Method:** Difference-in-differences comparing students aged 11–19 with the rest of the population in a window around March 2, with region, occupation, year and month fixed effects, plus an event study of daily effects.

**Finding:** Suicide deaths among students rise significantly after March 2, with no comparable increase in the rest of the population.

**Data:** Individual-level mortality records, 1997–2023, from the Microdata Integrated Service (MDIS), Statistics Korea. The data is restricted and not included in this repository.

**File:** `ThesisAnalysis.do` produces the summary statistics, descriptive figures, regression table and event study.
